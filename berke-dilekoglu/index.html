01 — About

About Berke

I'm Berke Dilekoğlu (also written Berke Dilekoglu; berkedilekoglu on GitHub and Medium). I build NLP and large language model systems that run in production: table and field extraction from scanned documents, multi-class document classification, retrieval-augmented generation and agentic workflows.

Before moving into industry NLP I did deep-learning research on biological sequences at Sabancı University, where my MSc thesis was SUMOnet, a model for predicting SUMOylation sites. Since then I've worked on search ranking at Huawei, document-grounded chatbots at Ginoa.io and mortgage document AI at Areal.ai. I started BerkeML to take on client AI engineering and to ship my own AI-native products, such as FairyWhisper.
